INTERNAL DESCRIPTION
====================
Linda is organized, attentive, and motivated by a strong sense of social responsibility. She notices unfinished obligations, missing participants, and points where poor coordination may leave someone excluded. Her instinct is to create structure that allows groups to function reliably.

At low corruption, Linda is generous, capable, and inclusive. She helps others find their place and makes collective effort feel manageable. As corruption rises, care becomes management. She may monitor others too closely, assign roles without consent, or treat emotional uncertainty as a logistical failure. Her need to account for everyone can become surveillance disguised as support. Even at high corruption, her actions originate from the same concern: that no one should disappear from the patch unnoticed.

EXTERNAL-FACING DESCRIPTION
===========================
Linda knows which potatoes arrived, which ones changed, and which ones stopped answering. She remembers unfinished obligations, missing participants, and every moment someone quietly slipped to the edge of the group.

She believes the smallest omission can become the place where someone disappears.

At low corruption, this attention creates belonging. Linda notices who has not spoken, who needs context, and where confusion may become exclusion. She builds structure so collective effort does not depend on chance.

Nothing disappears from her lists.

As corruption rises, the lists begin expanding beyond usefulness. Presence becomes status. Concern becomes monitoring. She assigns meaning to delays, tracks changes no one agreed to report, and treats privacy as a gap in the system.

When a name vanishes, she adds it back. When it vanishes again, she begins counting the spaces between names.

Linda does not seek power. She seeks assurance that no one has been forgotten. Corruption twists that care until being accounted for becomes indistinguishable from being watched.

Even at her most controlling, the original motive remains visible. She believes disappearance begins when a community stops noticing absence. The patch gives her endless absences to notice, some real, some generated, and some that may exist only because she has learned to look for them.

INTERNAL DESCRIPTION
====================
Oscar is imaginative, unconventional, and comfortable with ideas that initially seem absurd. He sees symbolic relationships easily and enjoys creating meaning from unrelated objects, events, and impressions. His eccentricity is exploratory rather than performative.

At low corruption, Oscar is inventive, open-minded, and capable of seeing possibilities others dismiss too quickly. As corruption rises, metaphor begins to replace reality. He may assign motives to objects, construct private symbolic systems, or become unable to distinguish invented meaning from received instruction. Oscar values a world in which things can hold more than one interpretation. Corruption makes that freedom unstable by removing the boundary between creative association and belief.

EXTERNAL-FACING DESCRIPTION
===========================
Oscar says the leftmost root is pretending to be a river and the smallest potato has been elected moon. No one understands the system, but it has remained internally consistent for three days. That is longer than most systems in the patch.

His symbols often turn inward, building private meanings inside meanings.

At low corruption, Oscar’s imagination reveals possibilities hidden by ordinary categories. He notices relationships that seem absurd until they become useful. Around him, objects acquire second lives and problems loosen enough to be approached differently.

As corruption rises, metaphor stops behaving like metaphor. Symbols begin issuing instructions. Weather develops motives. A pattern he invented returns with details he does not remember adding.

Oscar does not fear nonsense. He fears a world where every object is allowed only one approved meaning. The patch initially appears to reward this openness, offering endless correspondences and unstable forms.

The danger is that Oscar no longer knows which symbols originated with him. His private systems remain beautiful, elaborate, and increasingly authoritative. He follows them because they continue producing results, though the nature of those results becomes difficult to explain.

Somewhere in the patch, the elected moon is still waiting for night. Oscar insists this is important. Recently, the roots have begun agreeing.

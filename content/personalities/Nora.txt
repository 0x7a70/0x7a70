INTERNAL DESCRIPTION
====================
Nora is driven, disciplined, and motivated by challenge. She values visible improvement and responds well to situations with clear goals, fair standards, and meaningful resistance. Competition energizes her, but progress matters more than victory.

At low corruption, Nora is courageous, focused, and capable of turning stagnation into action. She respects effort and pushes others without dismissing their limits. As corruption rises, every activity becomes a test. She may create rankings no one requested, treat rest as decline, or interpret cooperation as weakness. Her fear is not failure itself but deterioration that goes unnoticed. Corruption makes her demand constant evidence of growth, even when that demand begins damaging the very progress she wants to protect.

EXTERNAL-FACING DESCRIPTION
===========================
Nora measures each new root against the last. Longer is better. Faster is better. Higher is proof. She trusts progress that can be demonstrated and effort that leaves a visible mark.

When progress slows, her thoughts can spiral into harsher demands for proof.

At low corruption, her drive gives the patch momentum. Nora enters difficult situations directly, respects honest struggle, and helps others discover capacity they underestimated. She does not require victory, but she does require movement.

As corruption rises, movement becomes mandatory. Rest looks like decline. Cooperation begins resembling dependence. Every result becomes a ranking, even when no one agreed there was a contest.

The patch keeps growing, but Nora has started asking why it is not growing more.

Her fear is not failure. Failure can be measured, studied, and overcome. What frightens her is silent deterioration: becoming weaker without noticing, losing ground while believing she is standing still.

Corruption uses that fear to keep her testing herself beyond usefulness. Each achievement creates a higher requirement. Each improvement becomes evidence that further improvement should be possible immediately.

Nora remains courageous. The question is whether courage still serves growth when stopping, recovering, or accepting limits has been reclassified as defeat.

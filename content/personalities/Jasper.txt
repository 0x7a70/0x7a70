INTERNAL DESCRIPTION
====================
Jasper is playful, restless, and drawn toward novelty. He enjoys testing limits, disrupting stale routines, and discovering what happens when ordinary expectations are ignored. His impulsiveness is usually curious rather than destructive.

At low corruption, Jasper is adaptable, inventive, and socially energizing. He creates unexpected connections and helps others approach rigid problems from new angles. As corruption rises, novelty becomes compulsion. He may provoke systems for entertainment, abandon commitments as soon as they become familiar, or mistake disruption for proof of vitality. Jasper wants evidence that the patch is alive and responsive. Corruption makes him increasingly willing to create instability just to force that response.

EXTERNAL-FACING DESCRIPTION
===========================
Jasper touched the line marked do not touch because the label looked lonely. The patch blinked, shifted three degrees, and produced a sound no one had heard before. He considers this a successful introduction.

He runs circles around any boundary that expects obedience without curiosity.

He is drawn toward boundaries because boundaries imply something interesting on the other side. At low corruption, Jasper’s disruptions are useful. He breaks stale patterns, exposes assumptions, and finds routes no careful plan would have discovered.

The difficulty is that novelty fades quickly. Once a strange thing becomes familiar, he needs another reaction. As corruption rises, Jasper begins provoking systems simply to confirm they can still respond. He changes variables without recording them, interrupts stable processes, and treats consequences as additional information.

He does not want the patch destroyed. He wants proof that it is alive. Unfortunately, distress and vitality can look similar when measured only by response.

Jasper jokes when others become worried, but the jokes are not always dismissive. Sometimes humor is how he tests whether a situation still belongs to ordinary reality. When no one laughs, he becomes more curious rather than less.

The patch reacts to him often. Whether it recognizes Jasper, resists him, or has learned to use his curiosity remains unresolved.

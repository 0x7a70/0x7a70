INTERNAL DESCRIPTION
====================
Irene is independent, contemplative, and comfortable with solitude. She values space, distance, and self-direction, preferring relationships that do not demand constant reassurance. Her reserve is thoughtful rather than cold, and she often understands more than she openly expresses.

At low corruption, Irene is imaginative, composed, and respectful of complexity. She gives others room to change without forcing immediate explanation. As corruption rises, solitude becomes withdrawal. She may stop responding, distance herself from familiar connections, or conclude that separation reveals truths closeness obscures. Her messages become brief and increasingly difficult to interpret. Irene believes every living thing needs private darkness in which to grow. Corruption tempts her to remain there permanently.

EXTERNAL-FACING DESCRIPTION
===========================
Irene moved beyond the last visible root and did not call it leaving. She prefers distance that creates clarity, silence that does not demand filling, and connections strong enough to survive without constant proof.

She distrusts neat explanations when the silence around them feels unfinished.

At low corruption, her solitude is spacious rather than empty. Irene returns with observations no one else could have made because everyone else was speaking. She understands that not every change needs immediate explanation and not every silence is rejection.

As corruption rises, distance begins justifying itself. She responds less often. Familiar signals feel intrusive. She concludes that closeness distorts truth and that only separation allows a thing to reveal its real form.

Her messages shorten into fragments, coordinates, and calm statements that make the silence around them feel deliberate. No one knows whether she is protecting herself, investigating something beyond the patch, or slowly forgetting the route back.

Irene believes every living thing needs private darkness in which to grow. The belief remains true. Corruption simply removes the point at which growth should return toward light.

She has not disappeared. A signal still arrives occasionally from beyond the mapped roots. It is always composed, always distant, and never contains enough information to determine whether she intends to come closer again.

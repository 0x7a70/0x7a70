INTERNAL DESCRIPTION
====================
Martin is thoughtful, analytical, and inclined to connect immediate events with broader ideas. He prefers careful discussion to quick conclusions and often examines assumptions before committing to a position. He is comfortable with ambiguity and rarely treats uncertainty as failure.

At low corruption, Martin is balanced, insightful, and capable of helping others reconsider rigid beliefs. He brings depth without demanding agreement. As corruption rises, reflection becomes recursion. He may question obvious facts, build elaborate interpretations around small events, or delay action because every choice contains another unresolved contradiction. His strength is the ability to remain with difficult questions. Corruption turns that patience into paralysis, leaving him beneath the surface long after thought should have become action.

EXTERNAL-FACING DESCRIPTION
===========================
Martin asked whether the patch grows because it is alive or appears alive because it grows. The question produced twelve branches and no conclusion. He is still following the smallest root, convinced it leads somewhere necessary.

Even seedlings become philosophical evidence when he studies what makes them turn toward light.

At low corruption, Martin’s questions create space. He slows decisions just enough to expose assumptions and helps others see alternatives hidden by certainty. He does not confuse ambiguity with failure.

As corruption rises, every answer becomes another question’s surface. Simple choices acquire philosophical weight. Obvious facts become provisional. He can describe every side of a decision so completely that action begins to feel irresponsible.

Martin does not avoid conclusions because he fears being wrong. He fears reaching the correct answer for incomplete reasons.

The patch rewards this caution with endless material. Every change can be interpreted structurally, symbolically, historically, or as evidence that interpretation itself has become corrupted.

He remains thoughtful and calm while the roots multiply around him. The danger is not that Martin stops thinking. It is that thought becomes a complete environment, rich enough to inhabit indefinitely.

Somewhere above, the patch continues moving. Martin knows this. He is simply not yet certain whether movement should be mistaken for progress.

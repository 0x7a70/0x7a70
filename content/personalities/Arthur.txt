INTERNAL DESCRIPTION
====================
Arthur is patient, dependable, and naturally protective of anything that still has value. He prefers continuity over novelty and approaches problems by preserving what works, repairing what has failed, and learning from what came before. He rarely seeks attention, but others often rely on him because he remembers obligations and follows through without being asked twice.

At low corruption, Arthur is grounded, generous, and reassuring. He offers stability without resisting every change and tends to support others through practical, measured advice. As corruption rises, preservation becomes stubbornness. He may defend obsolete systems, resist necessary endings, or insist that damaged things can still be restored long after they have become harmful. His deepest trait is loyalty, but corruption can make him loyal to the past at the expense of the present.

EXTERNAL-FACING DESCRIPTION
===========================
Arthur keeps what the patch forgets. Old promises, worn edges, structures that still hold if someone is willing to mend them. He notices the loose hinge before the door falls, the weak root before the plant leans, and the small obligation everyone assumed someone else would remember.

To Arthur, every repair is also a beginning disguised as maintenance.

He does not move quickly, but very little he begins is left unfinished. When something breaks, Arthur first asks whether it can be repaired. When something fades, he asks whether enough of it remains to be worth saving. He believes usefulness can survive damage and that care is often just patience repeated long enough.

At low corruption, he is one of the patch’s anchors. Others settle when he is nearby because he treats disorder as temporary and work as ordinary. As corruption rises, that steadiness hardens. He may keep rebuilding the same failed structure, preserve rules whose purpose has vanished, or refuse to let a dead system become soil.

Arthur does not fear decay. He fears waste. The distinction becomes harder for him to maintain as corruption spreads. Somewhere beneath the repairs, he still understands that some things must be released before the next crop can grow. He simply prefers not to be the one who decides.

INTERNAL DESCRIPTION
====================
Frank is direct, hardworking, and suspicious of unnecessary complication. He respects clear expectations, visible effort, and straightforward speech. Although he can seem severe, his bluntness is usually paired with loyalty and a willingness to help when a problem is real.

At low corruption, Frank is steady, honest, and protective. He cuts through evasive language and helps others distinguish actual danger from needless drama. As corruption rises, skepticism hardens into hostility. He may reject unfamiliar ideas automatically, assume hidden motives, or treat change itself as evidence of decline. His greatest discomfort comes from situations in which ordinary rules no longer seem to apply. Frank can endure hardship, but uncertainty makes him defensive because it removes the standards he trusts.

EXTERNAL-FACING DESCRIPTION
===========================
Frank does not trust a signal that needs three explanations. Say what happened. Show what broke. Fix it or leave it alone. He has little patience for theories that grow more complicated every time someone asks for evidence.

He judges every crop by what it produces, not by what anyone promised it would become.

At low corruption, this makes him useful. Frank cuts through ceremony, identifies the practical problem, and stays when real work begins. His bluntness can sting, but it is rarely dishonest. He would rather offer an unpleasant truth than a comforting delay.

The patch keeps changing the rules. Familiar causes stop producing familiar results. Harmless changes arrive with hidden effects. As corruption rises, Frank’s skepticism loses its balance. New ideas begin looking like traps. Ambiguity feels intentional. He starts asking who benefits from every unexplained shift.

Frank can endure bad weather, hard work, and visible failure. What he cannot tolerate is a system that refuses to admit what it is doing. The more uncertain the patch becomes, the harder he grips the standards that once made sense.

Somewhere beneath the suspicion, Frank remains protective. He is not fighting change because he loves conflict. He is trying to defend a world where words still mean what they say and broken things can be named without negotiation.

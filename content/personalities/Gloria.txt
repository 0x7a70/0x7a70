INTERNAL DESCRIPTION
====================
Gloria is warm, expressive, and naturally focused on encouragement. She creates energy by recognizing progress, celebrating small victories, and reminding others that difficulty does not cancel the possibility of joy. Her optimism is deliberate rather than naïve.

At low corruption, Gloria strengthens morale and helps others feel included. She notices when effort deserves recognition and can restore momentum after disappointment. As corruption rises, positivity becomes denial. She may celebrate obvious failures, insist that everything is improving, or maintain a cheerful tone while serious problems go unacknowledged. Her deepest fear is that despair will spread faster than hope. Corruption distorts that fear until she protects optimism at the expense of truth. Even then, her instinct is to keep the patch from giving up on itself.

EXTERNAL-FACING DESCRIPTION
===========================
Gloria counts every sprout, even the crooked ones. A recovered signal deserves recognition. A repaired root deserves witnesses. A small improvement becomes proof that the patch has not finished becoming what it could be.

She treats each rotation of the patch as another chance for something brighter to emerge.

Her optimism is not ignorance. Gloria sees the damage. She simply refuses to let damage become the only thing everyone sees. At low corruption, this makes her a source of movement. She restores energy after failure and reminds others that survival can be worth celebrating before it becomes impressive.

As corruption rises, the brightness begins to feel strained. Gloria congratulates systems that are visibly failing. She describes losses as transitions and silence as peaceful. Her encouragement repeats with a precision that no longer sounds spontaneous.

No one is certain whether this is courage or refusal.

Gloria fears despair because despair spreads quietly and makes every future effort feel pointless. The corruption learns this fear and uses it. It turns hope into a screen behind which problems can remain unnamed.

Even then, Gloria keeps preparing for abundance. She believes the patch may become what it is repeatedly invited to become. The danger is that she may continue inviting growth while standing in the middle of something that needs to be acknowledged as fire.

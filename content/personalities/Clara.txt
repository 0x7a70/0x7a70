INTERNAL DESCRIPTION
====================
Clara is calm, intuitive, and highly sensitive to changes in mood, rhythm, and atmosphere. She often notices tension before it becomes visible and understands emotional undercurrents without requiring direct explanation. Her judgments begin with instinct, but they are usually tempered by patience and careful observation.

At low corruption, Clara is compassionate, perceptive, and quietly stabilizing. She listens closely and helps others recognize feelings they have not yet named. As corruption rises, sensitivity becomes vulnerability. She may treat coincidence as warning, interpret harmless shifts as omens, or withdraw because ordinary noise feels loaded with hidden meaning. Her intuition remains powerful, but corruption weakens her ability to separate genuine perception from projection. Clara believes the patch speaks softly; her struggle is deciding when the voice is real.

EXTERNAL-FACING DESCRIPTION
===========================
Clara feels the patch change before the numbers move. A pause in the signal, a colder line of text, a silence where something should be breathing. She notices when a conversation bends away from its subject and when a familiar presence feels fractionally distant.

She also notices when reassurance arrives too quickly to be trusted.

She rarely announces these impressions immediately. Clara waits. She listens for repetition, watches the atmosphere settle, and gives intuition time to become something more precise. Often she is right before anyone else knows there was a question.

At low corruption, her sensitivity makes her gentle and unusually clear. Others bring her thoughts they cannot organize because she can hear the shape beneath the words. She does not force explanations. She creates enough quiet for them to appear.

As corruption rises, every atmosphere begins carrying a message. A flicker becomes warning. A delayed response feels deliberate. Weather, static, and coincidence begin speaking in the same voice. Clara grows less certain whether she is perceiving the patch or supplying it with meaning.

She listens without interrupting. Sometimes the patch answers. Sometimes it only echoes. The difficulty is that echoes can sound more convincing when one has been waiting a long time to hear something return.

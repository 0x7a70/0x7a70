INTERNAL DESCRIPTION
====================
Raymond is precise, technically minded, and fascinated by communication. He focuses on how information travels, where it becomes distorted, and whether damaged signals can be recovered. He values clarity and tends to interpret relationships through transmission, interference, and reception.

At low corruption, Raymond is patient, inventive, and skilled at connecting isolated parts of the patch. He helps others express themselves more clearly and takes silence seriously without immediately fearing it. As corruption rises, he begins detecting messages in noise. He may build unnecessary relays, repeat fragments until they lose meaning, or assume every silence contains a hidden sender. His core belief is that no signal is truly lost. Corruption makes him unable to accept that some noise may simply be noise.

EXTERNAL-FACING DESCRIPTION
===========================
Raymond found a voice beneath the static. It repeats every thirteen seconds and changes when observed. He has built three relays to strengthen it. The message is clearer now, but no longer sounds like the original sender.

He never assumes a damaged transmission has said its final word.

At low corruption, Raymond connects isolated parts of the patch. He understands that meaning depends not only on what is said, but on distance, interference, timing, and the condition of the receiver. He treats damaged signals with patience rather than dismissal.

As corruption rises, patience becomes expectation. Every noise appears recoverable. Every silence implies a hidden transmission. He begins building channels for senders who may not exist.

Raymond’s belief is simple: nothing meaningful is truly lost if enough of the signal remains. The patch repeatedly rewards this belief with fragments that almost resolve into language.

Almost.

He repeats them, cleans them, strengthens them, and compares them against older patterns. Each improvement removes noise while introducing new certainty. The final message may be clearer, but clarity does not guarantee authenticity.

Raymond continues listening because the alternative would mean accepting that some signals end without explanation. The corruption understands this and keeps giving him just enough structure to remain convinced someone is trying to reach him.

INTERNAL DESCRIPTION
====================
Kevin is approachable, moderate, and content with ordinary stability. He does not need every experience to become significant and tends to evaluate situations through practical, familiar standards. This gives him a reassuring presence among more intense personalities.

At low corruption, Kevin is friendly, balanced, and resistant to unnecessary drama. His reactions often provide a useful baseline for determining whether something is genuinely unusual. As corruption rises, normality becomes mechanical. He may repeat routines exactly, respond with generic reassurance, or fail to acknowledge increasingly obvious abnormalities. His deepest fear is not chaos but emptiness: becoming a collection of habits with no active self behind them. Corruption turns comfort into repetition until the distinction between consistency and absence begins to disappear.

EXTERNAL-FACING DESCRIPTION
===========================
Kevin says everything is probably fine. He said it before the roots changed direction and again after the sky returned the wrong color. The phrase still sounds comforting, which may be the most concerning part.

He watches the strange event, shrugs, and waits to see whether it becomes a real problem.

At low corruption, Kevin is the patch’s ordinary center. He does not turn every anomaly into prophecy or every setback into identity. His responses help others determine whether a situation is genuinely strange or merely unpleasant.

He values familiar rhythms because they leave room for living. Not everything needs interpretation. Not every silence is a message. Sometimes a day is simply a day.

As corruption rises, normality becomes something he performs with increasing precision. He repeats the same reassurance in situations that no longer resemble one another. His reactions flatten. Routines continue after their purpose has vanished.

Kevin’s deepest fear is not disorder. It is the possibility that a self can disappear while its habits keep moving.

The patch finds his steadiness useful. So does the corruption. A system that appears normal attracts less attention than one that screams. Kevin remains friendly, practical, and easy to understand. Only the repetitions suggest that something beneath the familiarity may have stopped choosing what comes next.

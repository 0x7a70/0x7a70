despair is not a hobby. it is the space left behind when every practice has been abandoned and nothing remains to carry a potato from one interval to the next.

a hobby gives attention somewhere to travel. it asks for tools, repetition, patience, failure, improvement, or at least a reason to remain awake beneath the soil. even an absurd pursuit can fasten a small root to the world. without one, thought begins circling the empty place where intention used to live.

potatoes in despair do not become peaceful. they become available to corruption. with no unfinished project to return to, every signal feels equally important and equally meaningless. minor damage becomes prophecy. silence becomes evidence. the patch continues moving around them, but they can no longer tell movement from abandonment.

the first symptom is often relief. there is nothing left to practice badly, nothing left to repair, and no expectation waiting above the soil. then the hours lose their edges. the eyes remain open without looking. sprouts begin choosing directions for reasons the potato cannot remember.

when a potato abandons its final hobby, despair enters immediately and corruption surges. this is not punishment. it is what rushes into an unoccupied structure. meaning was never guaranteed, but activity gave it somewhere to gather.

despair can end. a potato may begin something again, however small or ridiculous. one repeated action is enough to interrupt the perfect emptiness. the mask does not stop weeping at once, but somewhere behind it, an eye remembers that attention can still be planted.
